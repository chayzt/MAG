01 · THE PITCH

Chayenne for Malaysia Airlines

Here is the honest truth about why I want this role.

I have spent my career building things that move Malaysians forward. At CIMB, that meant shaping financial engines that power millions of daily lives. At MRT Corp, it meant physically connecting communities across our capital. Both are, at their core, national backbones. I came home to build them on purpose, even as peers built careers abroad, because I have always believed Malaysia is where my work creates the greatest impact.

Malaysia Airlines is that very same instinct on the world stage: our premier flag carrier, our cultural identity, and our story shared with the globe. That is the mission I am applying to lead.

A James Craig Scholarship took me to the University of Manchester to read Economics and Social Science, where topping my class in year one secured a second tuition sponsorship. That academic rigour laid the foundation for everything that followed: analytical discipline, creative agility, and a relentless focus on bottom-line delivery.

Here is how that foundation translates into a high-impact commercial toolkit for MAG:
01 · FULL-STACK MARKETING

I lead marketing end-to-end, connecting brand equity to seat yield

I build and direct full-funnel marketing ecosystems spanning brand narrative, performance media, martech infrastructure and retargeting. In aviation, brand love must convert into seat occupancy; I bring the operational depth to connect daily route load factors directly to real-time performance campaigns across 26 global markets.

02 · COMMERCIAL ACUMEN

Financial discipline is where I started, and where I deliver

Marketing must be accountable to the P&L. Having built CIMB’s first Performance Marketing unit from the ground up to achieve 6X ROI across 7 million customer profiles, and subsequently expanding MRT Corp’s commercial revenue base by over 40%, I bring the commercial discipline needed to track and monetise every ringgit of marketing spend.

03 · MONETISING NATIONAL ASSETS

I turn cultural identity and public infrastructure into sustainable revenue

From major financial institutions to national rail transit, where I transformed urban stations into experiential live stages for global entertainment brands like Netflix and Sony Pictures, I know how to elevate pride into profit. Malaysia Airlines is our ultimate national asset, and I know how to unlock its non-fare and ancillary potential sustainably.

04 · CONTENT, PARTNERSHIPS & NETWORK

I architect strategic alliances that drive real commercial engagement

Strategic partnerships should never be just logos on a deck. From founding the SC WIN women’s network to designing co-branded initiatives like a prospective Zafigo partnership for female travel empowerment, I leverage an extensive network across chambers of commerce, enterprise brands, and global media. I build high-value barter models and hybrid inventory swaps that drive reach without depleting cash reserves.

To me, Malaysia Airlines represents both our sovereign pride and the deeply personal warmth of coming home. Bringing together full-stack marketing mastery, proven commercial acumen, and modern AI acceleration to drive sharper decisions at pace, I am ready to help our national carrier soar higher on the global stage.

90-DAY PLAN

Phased Execution

Phase 1 — Listen, Understand & Audit

DAYS 1–30
Marketing Team: Review campaign workflows, brand asset utilisation and content production pipelines.
Head of Commercial & RM: Map route booking curves, yield pressure points and real-time inventory triggers.
Media Agency & Social Listening: Assess Carat/Dentsu buying efficiency across 26 markets; audit passenger sentiment.
CX Team: Review post-flight satisfaction, lounge experience, inflight feedback and complaint trends.
Operations Team: Review OTP, baggage handling and turnaround realities so marketing promises match delivery.
Loyalty Team (Enrich): Audit non-air accrual/redemption rates, co-brand performance and member engagement.
Digital Officers & Retail: Review DSP checkout flow, app adoption and NDC corporate integrations.
HR (Talent Planning): Align hiring and capability roadmap with the new commercial operating model.
Airline CEO: Align on overarching vision, investment priorities and executive sponsorship for the 90-day plan.

Phase 2 — Design, Integrate & Automate

DAYS 31–60
Unified Commercial BI Dashboard: Single-pane tracking of Route PLF, RASK, Direct DSP Channel Share and Ancillary Yield.
Predictive Demand Alerts: AI flags soft forward bookings 30–60 days out, auto-triggering performance media.
Dynamic Creative Optimisation: AI-automated multi-language ad adaptation across 20+ countries.
Conversational Retargeting: AI-assisted prompts within Mavis to recover abandoned itineraries.
Segment Engine Launch: Dedicated packages for tertiary students and family milestone bundles.
Quarterly Reprioritisation: Apply a Start/Stop/Continue review every quarter to keep the roadmap sharp and evidence-led.

Phase 3 — Pilot, Scale & Govern

DAYS 61–90
Two-Corridor Pilot: Test RM-to-media steering on KUL–London (student/corporate) and KUL–Melbourne (VFR/leisure).
Live Brand Experience: Launch a physical cultural activation linking digital booking with real-world engagement.
Executive Dashboard Hand-off: Present the integrated commercial dashboard to CCO and commercial leadership.
Quarterly Agency JBP Governance: Formalise reviews with Dentsu, tying compensation to CPA and incremental seat yield.
SWOT REFERENCE

Commercial & Marketing SWOT

STRENGTHS

  • Renowned Hospitality Heritage: Strong brand love across ASEAN and global corridors.
  • Strategic Geographic Hub: KLIA offers natural East-West transit connectivity.
  • Oneworld & JV Network: Extended global reach via alliance partnerships.
  • Diversified Brand Portfolio: Distinct roles across MH, Firefly, MASwings and Amal.

WEAKNESSES

  • Legacy Cost Structure: Higher overheads vs. regional low-cost carriers.
  • Attribution & Data Gaps: Disconnect between top-of-funnel spend and DSP conversions.
  • Domestic Price Sensitivity: Heavy yield pressure on short-haul routes.
  • Distribution Costs: Lingering reliance on legacy GDS and high-commission intermediaries.

OPPORTUNITIES

  • Closed-Loop Media Steering: Shift Dentsu's 26-market spend to fill soft inventory in real time.
  • Life Milestone Demand: Own key life stages — student departures, family care, Hajj/Umrah.
  • Ancillary Retailing via NDC: Expand MHPlus, lounge access and seats direct to D2C.
  • Behavioural Loyalty: 30-day post-flight rebooking locks to capture repeat travel.

THREATS

  • Aggressive LCC Competition: Capacity expansion from AirAsia, Batik Air and Scoot.
  • Middle East Mega-Hubs: Gulf carriers siphoning long-haul transit traffic.
  • Macro Volatility: Exposure to jet fuel prices and USD/MYR fluctuations.
  • Supply Chain Constraints: Aircraft delivery and maintenance backlogs.
